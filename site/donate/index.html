About Kimberley

From an early age, Kimberley’s family instilled in her a love of community and a desire to make the world a better place. From her background in Environmental Science and competitive rowing, to earning a Red Seal in joinery and raising her family in Langford, her diverse experiences have taught her the value of practical, hands-on problem-solving.

Since being elected to Langford Council in 2022, she has worked hard to modernize the city while keeping a sharp focus on fiscal accountability. Hearing the real concerns of residents facing tough tax environments, she is committed to balancing the initiatives that build community and make people feel connected, with cost discipline; ensuring Langford remains a place where families can afford to stay and thrive for years to come.
