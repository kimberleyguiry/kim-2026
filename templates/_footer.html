About Kimberley

From an early age, Kimberley’s family instilled in her a love of community and a desire to make the world a better place. That early calling shaped her academic path in environmental science and was further deepened through competitive rowing, where she learned firsthand the power of teamwork and shared purpose. After getting married and moving to Vancouver Island, she earned her Red Seal certification in joinery, and began building a life and raising a family in Langford.

Her deep commitment to local connection grew through various community roles before stepping up to serve on Langford Council. Since being elected, she has poured her energy into championing inclusive policies and making smart, affordable choices for the city. Her passion for community is a thread that meaningfully ties her life experiences together and it is what is pushing her to continue serving today.
