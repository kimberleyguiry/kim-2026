Doing the work that I promised

In 2022, you trusted me to put people first and bring balance back to a city growing too fast; where infrastructure, amenities, and your voice were being left behind.

We inherited challenges that demanded careful consideration and hard decisions, and I am grateful for your trust in me to put our city’s long-term health first. Now the hardest part is behind us and we’ve successfully laid the foundation for a better future. Here’s what that looks like:

Bringing Langford to the regional table

Langford used to sit on the sidelines of regional decisions. By serving as a member and leader on key regional boards and commissions, I’ve helped bring us to the table to collaborate and deliver real wins for our community

  • Childcare: Helped establish the new Childcare and Community Arts Centre on Glen Lake Road.
  • Youth Mental Health: Helped launch Foundry West Shore and championed stable funding for youth crisis support (MYST).
  • Recreation: Advanced the West Shore’s first Master Recreation Plan
  • Water Infrastructure: Secured decades of safe drinking water.
  • Public Safety: built new CREST operations and public safety building
  • Regional Services: Established new regional transportation and performing arts services

What I’m fighting for

A city that plans for tomorrow

  • Stable taxation and financial resiliency
  • Protecting our natural assets/parks
  • Proactive investment in public safety
  • Long term vision on infrastructure

A community where you belong

  • Building community through sports, arts, culture and placemaking
  • Streets that feel good to be on, and a city people want to explore
  • Connected communities attract skilled neighbors ready to give back.

A city that works for you

  • Transportation options to meet diverse needs
  • Supporting youth with safe routes, arts, and sports
  • Housing opportunities that meet needs
  • Supporting local business at every stage of growth
  • A government that works hard to be efficient and effective
  • Easier access to government information
