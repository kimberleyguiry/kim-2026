On the issues

Fixing the Gridlock Through Smart Planning
Growth shouldn't mean endless traffic jams. We need to link how land is used directly with transportation planning, so that infrastructure and service investments reflect how people are living and working in any part of Langford.

Real Options for Getting Around
A growing city needs safe, practical mobility choices. Whether people are walking, taking transit, or driving, everyone deserves comfortable, affordable ways to get around in the way that works for them.

Tackling Traffic at a Regional Level
Langford doesn't exist in a bubble. We must have strategic, consistent conversations with the Ministry of Transportation, BC Transit, the CRD, the Island Corridor Foundation, and the Sooke School District to secure safe routes to school and regional connectivity.

Protecting Regional Affordability
Langford has always been the region’s affordable place to live, and that identity shouldn't change. Keeping housing costs low and removing unnecessary barriers has always been a core value for our city.

Fostering Balanced Growth
City hall should maintain a building and innovation-friendly culture to ensure we keep creating vital, affordable housing options. At the same time, we have a duty to current residents to build complete communities where people truly want to live, ensuring new growth feels natural and welcome rather than overwhelming.

Smart Tax Decisions for the Long Term
Affordability must guide every tax conversation. We need to face today's challenges head-on so that our tough decisions keep costs low for families both right now and for years to come.

Real Support for Today's Youth and Families
A city that supports its youth is a city that supports its future. Being a youth in today's world comes with more challenges than ever before. We need strong social services for youth and their families to meet the complex needs of folks growing up in today's world.

Diverse and Healthy Opportunities
Young people need a variety of options to connect with each other and their community, which means actively removing barriers to sports, arts, culture, and access to safe, welcoming public spaces.

Honoring and Supporting Seniors
A thriving community looks out for all generations. Seniors are irreplaceable and deserve accessible recreation, art, culture, and social connection tailored to diverse abilities and fixed incomes.

Accessibility and Affordability for All
Making these opportunities affordable, safe, and easy to reach is essential to supporting youth, families, seniors, and every corner of our diverse community.

Equitable Access to Nature
Having nature on your doorstep is part of what makes Langford special. Everyone should have access to greenspaces, whether living right in the urban City Centre or out near our rich regional and provincial parks.

Protecting and Growing Our Canopy
Protecting what we have and making strategic investments in how we grow greenspaces is an integral part of ensuring we continue to have the necessary greenspace and tree canopy for a happy and healthy growing city.

Investing in Local Recreation
Recreation is vital for health, well-being, and social connection. We need to make strategic, equitable investments in recreation infrastructure that genuinely work for the people using them.

Regional Partnerships and Community Groups
By collaborating across the West Shore and partnering with local community and sport groups, we can deliver programs and build connections that truly meet the needs of west shore communities.

Prioritizing Real Safety in Our Neighborhoods
Public safety has a huge impact on how we feel about where we live. When we feel safe about where we live, then we feel safe about being out in the community with our families and loved ones, making connections and being a part of a larger community.

Backing Our First Responders
We must continue investing in essential professionals like police officers and firefighters who protect our community every day.

Smart Design to Prevent Crime
Safer streets start with proactive infrastructure. Better street lighting, traffic calming, and thoughtful public design discourages illegal activity and vandalism before it happens.

Supporting Effective Responses
Consistent bylaw enforcement is crucial to keeping public spaces clean, orderly, and welcoming for everyone. Choosing the right safety response for the right safety challenge is effective and keeps costs down.
