Bringing Langford to the regional table

Langford used to sit on the sidelines of regional decisions. By serving as a member and leader on key regional boards and commissions, I’ve helped bring us to the table to collaborate and deliver real wins for our community

  • Childcare: Helped establish the new Childcare and Community Arts Centre on Glen Lake Road.
  • Youth Mental Health: Helped launch Foundry West Shore and championed stable funding for youth crisis support (MYST).
  • Recreation: Advanced the West Shore’s first Master Recreation Plan
  • Water Infrastructure: Secured decades of safe drinking water.
  • Public Safety: built new CREST operations and public safety building
  • Regional Services: Established new regional transportation and performing arts services
