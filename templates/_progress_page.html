The city at street level

Improving Safe Routes to School

2024 budget decisions shifted the use of the General Amenity Reserve Fund away from unsustainable tax subsidization back to the building amenities like safe routes to school. Over $15 million from various funds has been used to build sidewalks, protected bike lanes & multi-use paths, traffic calming and intersection improvements near schools.

Master Transportation Plan and Active Transportation Plan

Growing our city thoughtfully by connecting land use and transportation planning means better outcomes for how we get around. Mobility choices mean more freedom to move around in a way that works for you and your budget.

Ending future use of plastic grass

Once upon a time, new boulevards automatically meant installing artificial turf. Removing this requirement has meant decreased maintenance costs and more natural beauty on our city streets.

Services that make our community better

Doctors, right where we need them

In partnership with the South Island Primary Care Society, this new medical facility is home to 10 physicians. Since it is located in Langford City Centre, there are many transportation options available making sure these new doctors are also easy to get to.

Improved fire safety through investment

The addition of 27 firefighters over 3 years means that Fire Hall #2 is now operating 24/7. This improves response times and safety for the whole city meaning that residents, firefighters and property all have more protection.

Free youth health and wellness services

Coupled with a new childcare centre and 73 childcare spaces, the new Foundry West Shore will be built in the heart of Langford, ensuring youth health and wellness services are free, confidential and accessible.

New Community Arts and Childcare Centre

Located by Starlight Stadium, this two story building will be home to 49 licensed childcare spaces and the newly updated West Shore Parks and Recreation Arts and Culture Centre complete with spaces for pottery, music, art, dance and community gathering.
